CIRCUITBRIDGE ELECTRONICS - FICTIONAL BUSINESS PROFILE (student demo)

CircuitBridge Electronics is a fictional online retailer that helps students, makers, electronics laboratories and small engineering teams choose suitable hardware. Our mission is to make technical shopping understandable: customers should know exactly what a product does and whether it fits their project before requesting a purchase. NOVA, our virtual technical sales engineer, helps customers compare the store's demo catalog, discuss specifications, receive non-binding quotations, and request a human follow-up.

The store's demo catalog includes microcontroller boards, prototyping equipment, sensors, oscilloscopes, soldering tools, bench power supplies, USB-C power accessories, Ethernet switches, and wireless networking hardware. The authoritative product names, exact specifications, product identifiers and USD demo prices are in product_catalog.json. Prices and product descriptions are invented solely for this coursework; there is no real inventory, payment processing, live ordering, or product warranty contract.

Demo store policies: all catalog prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes, customs, shipping, and installation. The bot can calculate item subtotals, but must not invent a shipping charge or a total including unknown taxes. Stock levels, actual delivery lead times, accepted payment methods, shipping destinations, discounts, warranty duration, and compatibility not explicitly documented are unconfirmed. If a customer asks something not supported by the business profile, summary, or catalog, NOVA clearly says it cannot verify the answer, records the unanswered question using record_feedback, and offers a human follow-up.

The fictional business team consists of Dana Rahim (Founder and Technical Director, embedded systems), Elias Haddad (Product Engineer, networking and test equipment), and Noor Karam (Customer Experience Lead). The unique value proposition is engineering-first guidance, clear technical specifications, transparent demo pricing, and helpful quotations without sales pressure. Customers can optionally leave their name, email and inquiry so a team member could follow up in a real implementation. For this demo, the information is logged locally to private JSONL files rather than sent as actual messages.
